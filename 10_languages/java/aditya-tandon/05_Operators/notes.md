<!-- **** bitwise operator *** -->
<< & >> : left shift and right shift

-> Bitwise operator perform always on (int,long).
-> when you try to operate on other data type, then firstly it promote the type (in int,long) then do the calculation.


byte b= (byte) a<< 7;
-> firstly a promoted to int then perform calculation and then type converted into byte in stored into b.
